pybosl2.vectors.vector_axis(a, b)[source]¶

Return the axis vector (cross product) and angle between a and b.

The axis is the unit vector perpendicular to both a and b (requires 3-D vectors). The angle is the result of vector_angle().

Parameters:

  • a (Point) – First 3-D vector.

  • b (Point) – Second 3-D vector.

Returns:

A (axis, angle) tuple where axis is a unit 3-D list and angle is in radians.

Raises:

ValueError – If vectors are not 3-D or have zero length.

Return type:

tuple[list[float], float]
